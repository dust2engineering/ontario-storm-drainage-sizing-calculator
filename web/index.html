Ontario Storm Drainage Sizing Calculator

Roof area × SB-1 rainfall → leader and storm building drain sizes, from OBC Part 7 Tables 7.4.10.9 and 7.4.10.11.

Ontario sizes storm drainage in litres of hydraulic load: roof area (m²) times the maximum 15-minute rainfall (mm) from Supplementary Standard SB-1 — about 28 mm across much of the GTA, but confirm your municipality. Leaders come from Table 7.4.10.11 and storm building drains from Table 7.4.10.9 by slope.
