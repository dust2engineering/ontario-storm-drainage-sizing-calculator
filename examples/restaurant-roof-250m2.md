# Example 2 — Small restaurant roof, 250 m2, 28 mm rain, drain slope 1 in 50

Hydraulic load = 250 x 28 = 7,000 L.
- Leader: NPS 4 (10,800 L).
- Storm building drain at 1 in 50: NPS 5 (10,800 L). At 1 in 200 the NPS 5 and NPS 6 rows have no
  permitted value at all and the answer jumps to NPS 8 (18,900 L) — slope changes the answer, which is
  why the table is organized by slope.
